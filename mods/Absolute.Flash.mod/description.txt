The mod adds the Absolute Flash character and a few others.


Abilities:

F (Head) – Time dilation.
H (Head) – Whole‑body vibration, allowing you to pass through objects.
R (Forearm) – Lightning charge shot.
H (Forearm) – Hand vibration, dealing damage to surrounding enemies.
J (Forearm) – Visual vortex that repels and destroys objects.
F (Torso) – Creation of clones.
H (Torso) – Massive attack (barrage fire).
